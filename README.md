# Lord of the Mysteries
## Season 1: Clown
### Synopsis
Story follows **Klein Moretti**, a young man who wakes up in another world. Follow him as he discovered the world set in a Victorian era inspired world where magic and supernatural powers exist. Beneath the surface of the world hidden organizations and evil entities plot against the world. It is now Klein's job to stop them and maybe even return home.

##Main Characters
- Klein Moretti
- Melissa Moretti
- Dunn Smith
- Old Neill
- Leonard Mitchell
- Alger Wilson
- Audrey Hall

##World Setting
- **Beyonders**-People who obtain abilities through drinking potion. They are the people on *The path to divine*.
- **Sequence**-The levels of advancement of beyonder starting from 9 to 0.
- **Potions**-The item where beyonders get their power. Potions are made of rare materials and monster parts and can be consumed as is or a ritual needs to be done first.
- **Pathways**-Different routes of advancement for beyonders. There are 22 pathways with different abilities from each other.
